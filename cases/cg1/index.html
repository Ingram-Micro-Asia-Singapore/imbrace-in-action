    4How it's built

    What was built in iMBrace

    Data

    Three DataIQ boards:

    • Altavia Brand Rules: 25 rules, each with reference, kind, value, wrong forms, length limit and status. Marketing edits them without touching a workflow.
    • Campaign Briefs: brief reference, status, campaign, product, audience, goal, offer, call-to-action link, approved facts, requester notes and draft, then the written copy, brand check, left-out requests and approval.
    • Campaign Content Decisions: brief reference, decision, note, outcome.

    Logic

    1. Changing a brief's status to Submitted starts the workflow through a board automation. Fixed.
    2. Code reads the brand rules and checks the requester's own draft.
    3. AI writes email, LinkedIn and SMS copy from the brief's approved facts.
    4. Code runs the brand check and names each problem.
    5. AI fixes exactly those problems, then code checks again, up to two revisions.
    6. The copy, its check and any requests left out are written to the brief, and the run pauses at FlowOps' Approval step.
    7. A decision added on the decisions board starts a second workflow. Code checks the brief and version are the ones held, then resumes the run and records the outcome.

    Both workflows were built in FlowOps.

    AI setup

    One agent, Campaign Content Writer, created in AgentIQ on DeepSeek V3.2. Its Personality and Tone & Style come from the brand guide, its Banned Words list holds the guide's ten words, and temperature is 0.3. It has one skill, built in FlowOps, that reads the active brand rules. Its instructions limit it to the brief's approved facts and say it cannot approve, publish or send.

    How people reach it

    Marketing enters briefs and decisions in DataIQ forms; writing the copy involves no chat. Once a campaign is approved, partners can respond to it in iMBrace's hosted web chat, where a separate FlowOps workflow records their interest and replies with a lead reference.
