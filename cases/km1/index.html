    4How it's built

    What was built in iMBrace

    Data

    • Knowledge folder "HR Policy Library": nine policy PDFs, one per policy and version. Each states its status (current or superseded), version, effective date and numbered sections.
    • DataIQ board "HR Questions Queue": Ref, Status, Question, Answer, Note, Asked at, Asked via. This is HR's work list.
    • DataIQ board "HR Approved Answers": Ref, Status, Question, Answer, Approved at, Note. This is the only set of HR replies the assistant may quote.

    Logic

    1. AI searches the policy folder and the approved-answers board, and answers from the current version, citing document, version and section.
    2. If nothing covers the question, AI calls the "Log Questions for HR" skill, a FlowOps flow.
    3. Fixed flow reads the queue, numbers the question and adds it as an open record.
    4. HR writes an answer in the queue and sets Status to Approved.
    5. A board automation starts the "Publish Approved HR Answers" flow, built in FlowOps.
    6. Code re-reads the record, checks it is approved and copies it once to HR Approved Answers.
    7. If HR later sets Status to Withdrawn on that board, the answer is no longer given.

    AI setup

    One AgentIQ general agent, the HR Policy Assistant, running on deepseek.v3.2 at temperature 0.1. Its Core Task rules say to use only current documents, cite sources, and say so plainly when nothing covers a question. Its supporting knowledge is the HR Policy Library folder plus the HR Approved Answers board. The open questions queue is deliberately left out, so unanswered questions are never quoted. Its only skill is "Log Questions for HR", plus iMBrace's own knowledge search.

    How people reach it

    Employees ask in the InsightsIQ internal assistant screen. HR works directly in the DataIQ queue board, editing the record and changing its Status.
