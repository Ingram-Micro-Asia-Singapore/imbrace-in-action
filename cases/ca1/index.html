    4How it's built

    What was built in iMBrace

    Data

    Five DataIQ boards:

    • Daily Sales: Date, Region, online and wholesale revenue, cost of goods, returns. There are 420 records, loaded from a CSV with the board's Import.
    • KPI Alert Rules: Ref, KPI, Z threshold, Material change, Unit, Noise floor, Status, Note. The analyst edits these.
    • KPI Check Requests: one record per check, with the date to check, Status, Alert ref and Result.
    • Sales Alerts: one record per alert, with its summary, drivers, explanation and Disposition.
    • Alert Precision: the running share of reviewed alerts marked material.

    Logic

    1. Someone adds a record on KPI Check Requests. A board automation starts the "Detect Sales Anomalies" flow, built in FlowOps.
    2. The flow re-reads the request, then reads Daily Sales and the rules.
    3. Code compares the day with the usual eight-week average and splits any change into its drivers, such as online and wholesale.
    4. Code decides whether anything is worth alerting. On a normal day the flow ends with "No anomaly" and makes no AI call.
    5. AI words the facts as a short explanation.
    6. Code checks every number in the wording against the calculation.
    7. The alert and the result are written back.
    8. When someone sets a Disposition on the alert, a second board automation starts "Record Alert Dispositions", which updates Alert Precision.

    AI setup

    One AgentIQ general agent, the Sales Anomaly Analyst, on deepseek.v3.2 at temperature 0.1, memory off, replies within four sentences. It has no skills and no attached knowledge, because it only words facts it is given. It is called from the AI step inside the check flow.

    How people reach it

    Entirely on DataIQ boards. Staff add requests and edit rules or dispositions in the record form.
